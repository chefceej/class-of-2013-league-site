v2.8.0

August 11, 2026
  • Start Planner now shows two matchup weeks for the playoffs, each in its own collapsible section so the current week stays front and center
  • Next week's starts are rotation-projected (each starter's turn rolled onto their team's real schedule) since ESPN only posts probables a few days out — projected starts are marked with a ~ and firm up as the week nears
  • Each week tracks its own start count against the cap

v2.7.1

August 10, 2026
  • Standings no longer stop updating once the playoffs begin — the daily refresh was crashing on the first bye week
  • Final regular-season standings now include the last day of games

v2.7.0

June 24, 2026
  • Bulk Relievers board (in Streaming section): overlooked free-agent relievers who work 1.1–3.0 innings, ranked by points per inning
  • Each reliever flagged Due or Rested based on their usual rest between appearances
  • Pitchers who've shifted into a starting role (last 3 outings over 3.0 IP) are filtered out automatically
  • Header tabs now scroll sideways on mobile so every page is reachable
  • Free Agent Targets section split into Starting Pitchers and Bulk Relievers tabs — no more scrolling past one to reach the other
  • Bulk Relievers table is now sortable by any column (Pitcher, Status, Last App, Pts/IP)
